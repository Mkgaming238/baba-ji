Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What this site collects

To generate a Kundli (birth chart), this site asks for a name, date of birth, time of birth, and birth place. These are used only to calculate your astrological chart and are processed in your browser and, for AI-powered interpretation and chat features, sent to Google's Gemini API via our server.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not store your birth details on our servers beyond what is needed to process a single request.
  • We do not require an account or login to use the core features.

Third-party services

This site may use:

  • Google Gemini API — to power AI chat and chart interpretation. Data sent to it is subject to Google's API Terms.
  • Google AdSense — to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Cookies

Advertising partners (including Google) may set cookies in your browser to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can use this site without providing any birth details by exploring features like daily horoscope and Tarot, which don't require personal information beyond a zodiac sign selection.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner via the contact details provided on the site.
